HOW TO PLAY

Driving (Casual)

The car speeds up, brakes for corners and drifts on its own. You steer: hold the left or right half of the screen, tilt the phone (Settings), use ← → / A D, or a gamepad stick.

Drift: steer hard into a fast bend. Drifting fills the blue boost bar; link drifts quickly for a x2–x5 combo. Hitting a wall mid-drift loses it.

Boost: tap both sides of the screen together twice, Space / ↑, or A on a gamepad. Boost just before GO for a perfect start.

Slipstream: sit right behind a rival to fill the white draft bar; when it's full you get a free slingshot.

Driving (Pro)

Settings → Driving → Pro: ↑/W accelerate, ↓/S brake, Space handbrake (drift), Shift boost. On a phone, pedals appear on screen; on a gamepad the triggers are the pedals.

Modes

Quick race (pick the track, layout, day/night, dry/rain, 1 or 2 players), Time trial (race your ghost, share it as a code, watch replays), Cups (points over 3–4 races), Career (20 events, bosses, a daily challenge and trophies).

Two players

Race setup → 2 players: player 1 uses A/D + W, player 2 uses ← → + ↑, or plug in two gamepads.

Keys: C camera · P pause (photo mode is in the pause menu) · R back on track · M mute.
